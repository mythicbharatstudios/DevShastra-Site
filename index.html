Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Who we are

DevShastra is made and published by Mythic Bharat Studios. This policy explains what information the DevShastra Android app uses and why. The same text is shown inside the app under Settings → Privacy policy, so it is readable offline.

Your learning data stays on your phone

Your progress, answers, streaks, XP, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. DevShastra has no user accounts in this version and does not send your learning data to us.

Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Reset all progress, deletes it.

Internet connections

The app downloads the list of upcoming coding contests from the public Codeforces API (codeforces.com) and may download new questions from our content server. These requests don't include any personal information. The servers receive standard technical data such as your IP address.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location based on IP address, and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads.

Where the law requires it — for example in the EU and UK — the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy options. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

DevShastra Premium is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. The app only receives whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

The daily reminder is optional and off by default. It is created on your phone; no data is sent anywhere to show it.

Children

DevShastra is designed for college students and adults preparing for technical careers. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the app changes — for example, when optional accounts or leaderboards are added — and change the date above.
